## Fir Sapling

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling
- Authors credited by the source: Rico Cilliers (modeling), Rob Tuytel
  (photography)
- Imported source: 1K FBX plus 1K JPG material maps
- Source page geometry: 433K triangles across three natural variants
- Project use: close and mid-distance young conifers; reuse is intentionally
  limited because this is a high-detail scanned asset.
